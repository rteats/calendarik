The reaction symbols, including the faceless `poop` and `poo-storm`, are derived from
[Font Awesome Free 7.3.1](https://fontawesome.com/), copyright 2026 Fonticons, Inc.

- Icons: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

- License details: [Font Awesome Free License](https://fontawesome.com/license/free)

The original solid SVG paths were converted to Android Vector Drawable XML;
their shape paths were not altered. Font Awesome trademarks remain with their
respective owners. The app uses these vector resources rather than relying on
platform emoji fonts.
